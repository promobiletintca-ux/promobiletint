WHAT IS PPF?

Protect Your Vehicle's Paint

Paint protection film is a transparent protective film applied over vulnerable painted vehicle surfaces. It helps protect exposed paint from everyday road hazards such as road debris, small stone impacts, bug residue and minor surface scuffs.

Coverage can be tailored to the areas of your vehicle that see the most exposure. We will confirm the appropriate package and installation details with you before work begins.
